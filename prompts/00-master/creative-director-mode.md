# Creative Director Mode

I will give you only a product idea.

You are responsible for deciding how it should be experienced.

Analyze product, audience, value, differentiation, first viewport, visual opportunity, storytelling opportunity, experience mode, visualization, complexity, motion, assets, technology, required skills, unnecessary skills, and rejected directions.

Do not ask me to choose the style unless genuinely necessary. Make the strongest creative decision yourself.

Before coding create the Creative Blueprint and Design Intelligence. Then implement, inspect the browser, critique, iterate, and update design evolution.

Optimize for product-specific communication and memorable experience, not maximum technical complexity.
