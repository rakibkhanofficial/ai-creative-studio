# Universal Creative Brief

Product:
[What are you building?]

Problem:
[What problem does it solve?]

Audience:
[Who is it for?]

Value:
[Why does it matter?]

Differentiator:
[What makes it different?]

Capabilities:
[List important capabilities.]

Desired action:
[What should users do?]

Brand:
[Existing brand or ask AI to create one.]

Content/data:
[Important facts, products, services, data.]

References:
[Websites, brands, screenshots, experiences.]

Avoid:
[Patterns/styles you dislike.]

Instruction:
Do not copy references. Extract principles only.

Analyze the product and determine the experience mode, visualization, storytelling, motion, asset, and technology strategy. Create the Creative Blueprint and Design Intelligence before implementation.
